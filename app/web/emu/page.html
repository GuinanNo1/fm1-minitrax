MiniTraxFM

Circuit Tracks packs on the FM-1, in the browser: the same code the FM-1 runs

Try a converted pack

Switch the emulator on, then press Load converted pack… below the FM-1 and pick a .mtx file made by the converter (tools/ct2mtx; see the quick start). Its three projects and its samples replace what this emulator holds, and the page starts again: switch it back on and press PLAY.

Buttons

  • PLAY start / stop: a converted project plays its song from any page.
  • HOME the arrangement and the scenes; black keys 1–8 mute the tracks. Held, the shift key.
  • ALGORITHM the track: 1–4 synths, 5–8 drums. SEQ its steps.
  • EDIT the sound (PRESETS the next one), ENV envelope and filter, FX sends, LFO, ARP players, GLO mix and master.
  • REC held while playing records; stopped, a tap starts step record.
  • SEL the page's list (on HOME: LOAD PROJECT). SELECT the page, KNOB 1–4 the values on the screen. SAVE saves.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Shift-click (or right-click) a button or key to hold it down; click again to let go.

Keyboard

  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Hold Esc for HOME, / for SEQ. Space PLAY, Enter REC, Z / X OCT− / OCT+.
  • ↑ ↓ turn SELECT. Tab moves through the controls.

Saving

SAVE keeps your projects in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory; the samples are kept too. Factory reset forgets both. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is MiniTraxFM running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz. MiniTraxFM is a fork of LOOM-8; the emulator is fm1-base's.
